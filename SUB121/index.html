Smashing subs, taking dubs, and spreading love is our 2026 new years resolution. Sub 49 Records is PROUD to present our first release of the year; “Bass Damager” by Chuck Shadow x Dread MC is the filthy banger that all club systems yearn for. But good things take time. This track was started 4 years ago by Chuck Shadow x Dread MC and it has been sitting on our shelf for almost a year now. After countless revisions, feedbacks, experiments, blood, sweat, and tears, it’s finally time to release this masterpiece to the world. And it’s safe to say that all of us couldn’t be more PROUD of this record.

“Bass Damager” is the perfect cross between UKG and Bass House. With a bass that smacks you in the face, and vocals that lock you in with grace. Perfect for peak time dance floors or closing sets at festivals. Every DJ is going to want to add this to their arsenal. Chuck Shadow and Dread MC need no introductions; with releases on Night Bass, Crucast, 3000 Bass, and many many more. Their accolades are too long for this bio… so be sure to follow them on their socials.

Please enjoy responsibly, and thank you for being a part of Sub 49 World Wide.
