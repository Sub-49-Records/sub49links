Death smiles at all of us, only the brave smile back. Sub 49 Records is proud to welcome PHANTASM to the Sub 49 family. Phantasm is a horror inspired producer with 11 years in the music industry. Formerly known as 'Achilles' with releases on UKF, Space Yacht, Gold Digger, Bite This! and more, his new project 'Phantasm' brings gritty bass house, dubstep, and DnB to the forefront of the electronic music scene. Based out of Boston, Massachusetts, His production has earned him national tours as well as induction into the world renowned 3000 Bass as an A&R Member. His track 'WISH TO DIE' is an in your face Bass House banger. Fat horns, pumping baselines, and crunchy drums; fitting for peak time festival dance floors or the perfect backdrop while revolting against a tyrant. Horrifying sounds from the depths of hell, please help us welcome Lucifer's right hand man. PHANTASM

Please enjoy responsibly, and thank you for being a part of Sub 49 World Wide.
