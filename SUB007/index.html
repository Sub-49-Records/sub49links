Sub 49 introduces the Gangster EP, a fusion of two different worlds. The sharp, hard-hitting g-house of GRAYMATTER combines with the dark, melancholy sounds of Minor. They joined forces with Taz Zingale, an east coast rapper with humble beginnings and forthright lyrics that reflect his individuality. The result is edge, swagger, and darkness wrapped up into one. So lace up your boots, and get ready for some fat beats that will send you to the streets.
