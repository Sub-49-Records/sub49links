We’re all potential criminals; It’s just a matter of circumstance. In our case, some say we harbor the delinquent, but we say we give them a home. We’d like to welcome back criminal masterminds Average Citizens with their junior single titled “Bassline Criminal” along with a new addition to our Artist roster, KXNE from London, UK. Bassline Criminal feat. KXNE is a smooth criminal, choosing groovy over heavy. It’s got all the bounce that garage has to offer, with just enough bass to give you a face. Average Citizens has been on a roll, recently being added to Spotify bangers editorial playlist while touring the world. Performing at dream festivals such as Shambhala, Electric Love, and more. KXNE is a veteran in the electronic music scene with an impressive resume. After rising up with BRANDON with their track titled “Shades On” via gold digger, clocking in a cool 5M streams, he’s been making a mark in history, releasing on industry leading labels such as Universal, Ministry of Sound, Repopulate Mars, Terminal Underground, and Confession just to name a few. He offers ghost writing, vocal packs, Voice overs and more under his brand name DXSHMUSIC.

Continuing this year’s journey of making releases more memorable, we have 2 special parties lined up for this release in collaboration with Seattle Paddle Raves and the French Guys Bakery.
