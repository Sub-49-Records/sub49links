Are we ready for something a little different? JPN brings a super stellar stick to our catalog with this release. This track is carried by a soft synth with a thumping drum line, along with a soulful vocal as a backdrop, and a sprinkle of hip hop adlibs to roll it all up into a fine tuned cane.

Haling from Ensenada, Baja California, Mexico, he started his music journey at just 7 years old. Now, 17 years later at the ripe age of 24, he has released on major labels such as 3000 Bass, Uprise Music, Future Cuts, G-Mafia Records, La Confrerie, and more.

Be sure to follow this promising young lad on his music journey, and let's give him a warm welcome into Sub 49.
