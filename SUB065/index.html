Returning for his 5th release, we welcome back Sub 49 Alum, Cheap Mind, and his newest single, 'Fresh.' Reminiscing over his first Sub 49 demo submission, we were blown away by his intricate and unique sound design that we have yet to hear replicated in modern House music. His new single, 'Fresh,' is no different. Combining meticulously crafted synth sounds with luscious pads and hard-hitting drums, Cheap Mind takes us on a psychedelic journey to the depths of experimental, Bass House music.

Please enjoy responsibly, and thank you for being a part of the Sub 49 Nation.
