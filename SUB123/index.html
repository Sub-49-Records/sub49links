When great minds align to create something dope, we can’t help but to bring it to life. We’ve been chasing the dopamine rush of supporting talented producers, And we got something that we’re head over heels for. Sub 49 Records is elated to welcome Aaron Jackson & Mac Milio to our discography with their track “Dopamine”. This Bass House track features soulful vocals with a drop that smacks you in the head, perfect for peak time dancefloors. Aaron Jackson is a legend in the Pacific North West after touring the world heavily from 2013-2015. He’s been signed to notable labels such as Monstercat, Confession, Box of Cats, 3000 Bass, DND Recs, Discovery Project, MixMASH, Heldeep. this ain’t bristol, and more! Mac Milio is a rising star from France who’s sound design and harmony driven arrangements blows competition out of the water. He’s released on Moretin, Gold Digger, Noir Sur Blanc, Box of Cats, Strange Smoke Records, and more! To celebrate this release, we’ll be hosting our 4th release and 5th release party of the year in Los Angeles on our quest to make releases more memorable for artists. Make sure to pre-save, RSVP, and we’ll see you in Los Angeles!

Please enjoy responsibly, and thank you for being a part of Sub 49 World Wide.
