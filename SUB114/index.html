You better take some time to check out this release. Sub 49 Records is proud to present “Takin’ My Time” by Labeeb. While attending Penn State University studying STEM, Labeeb fell in love with electronic music, eventually becoming president of the EDM Club. After graduating, he decided to launch the Labeeb project in 2023. Since then, he’s had 7 releases signed on notable record labels such as WyldeCard, Force of Habit, Storytime, Get Down Recs, and more! “Takin’ My Time” is a vibey summer banger. Featuring a solid bassline paired with luscious synths, soulful vocals, and vocal chops that swim around your head to create an atmosphere that makes you relive the best moments in your life.
