We're proud to present our latest single, 'There We Go', by one of our fellow Northwest producers, Houseium. Only having a little over 3 years of experience, Houseium has taken the electronic music world by storm, touting multiple major label releases in a very short amount of time. Those labels consist of: Night Bass, DnD Recs, House Call, Gold Digger, House Views, From The Hood and, now, Sub 49. With that being said, let's dive into this new single and give Houseium a warm, Sub 49 welcome!
