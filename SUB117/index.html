My Favorite Flavor’s you baby. It’s that time of the year again where co-owner and label manager of Sub 49 Records, James Gatz, drops a once a year tune. “Favorite Flavor” is a UKG inspired Future House track that challenges genres, making space for the unexplored side of Bass House. As he says, “The best part of being a label manager? Doing whatever the hell you want”. He loves to use the opportunity to create an experimental banger that might not be for all dance floors. As the news agency, Elated Society, once said, “James Gatz brings in a new era of 4/4 music by blending elements from multiple genres and pushing the boundaries of what is considered House and Techno in his production.”
