Santoor (suhn-TOOR) is a traditional Indian Instrument dating back to ancient times, with 100 strings that is a variation of the Iranian santur. Carlo Giannico's track 'Santoor' features a heavy kick drum, tribal percussions, sensual vocal, techno rap flow, along with a sinister but melodic Santoor.

Carlo Giannico, a 29-year-old producer from Taranto, Italy, boasts an impressive 10-year career in the electronic music scene. However, it was in 2023 that he launched his eponymous tech house project. Carlo has performed in iconic cities such as Las Vegas, Paris, Madrid, Barcelona, Montpellier, and Milan. He has garnered support from renowned artists including ACRAZE, Mau P, Don Diablo, and The Martinez Brothers. He has released on Bump N Grind, Baikonur Records, Groovy Bones, Rock Bottom Records, as well as Total Freedom Records. Let's give him a warm welcome into the Sub 49 Family.
