Don't be a fool and miss this one. We have a brand new Seattle artist coming into town. It's with great pleasure to add 'Foolery' by LEXDEEZ to the Sub 49 Catalog. You can hear the infectious percussion shine in this track, thanks to LEXDEEZ being formally trained as a drummer since he was young. He just had his debut release with Deep Bear Recordings, and he has more to come. Be sure to check him out and follow him on his musical journey!
