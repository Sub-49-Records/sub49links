Hailing from Pescara, Italy, Manaar started producing at the young age of 16. After years of experimentation, he, like us, fell in love with house music and never looked back. With noticeable releases on G-Mafia Records and Digital Empire Records, both of whom have plenty of our same alma mater, we welcome Manaar and his new song, 'Thinking Different,' to Sub 49. With a driving bass line, catchy vocal and an out-of-this-world, siren-like lead, this tune will surely turn some heads in our DJ sets.

Please enjoy responsibly, and thank you for being a part of the Sub 49 Nation.
