We're ready to welcome back Seattle's hottest DJ on the Block, Tony H, along with a new alumni, Spiro Catalano, to the Sub 49 catalog. Tony H has a resume that is outstanding to say the least. He is an ambassador for citizenM hotels, the Label Manager for Space Yacht (L.A.), as well as being the Label Head for Late Night Munchies and Munchies After Dark. Tony H has had releases on Desert Hearts, Dirtybird, Space Yacht, Wyldcard Records, Music Is 4 Lovers, Flashmob Records and much much more. Not only has he had many releases on countless labels, he's played some of the dopest parties across the country, and he's not letting off the gas. No wonder Kevin Knapp is on record saying Tony was an 'artist to watch' in 2021 and that held true in 2022 when he was put on '10 Rising Black Artists You Should Know' in Gray Area Magazine. Enjoy this collab EP titled 'Now or Later' with friends Spiro Catalano and Cha$e Bank$ to get an idea of what we're talking about.

Please enjoy responsibly, and thank you for being a part of the Sub 49 Nation.
