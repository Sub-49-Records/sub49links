We're taking it up a notch for our Sub 49 catalog. Introducing 'Higher' by DiscoTime & JaKuna, a dynamic collaboration between two talented artists from opposite sides of the globe. DiscoTime, hailing from New York City, brings his deep love for house music to the track, while JaKuna, a rising star from Santiago, Chile, infuses diverse musical elements and genre-bending sound into the mix.

DiscoTime has previously released with Sub49, showcasing his talent for crafting infectious beats that move the dance floor. JaKuna, on the other hand, has released with Candy Flip, Jendex Records, and Move Yourself, solidifying his position as a rising star in the tech/bass house scene.

'Higher' features classic piano chords that dance around a soulful vocal, a wide gritty reese bass, and a thumping bassline that sits on a punchy groove. This track will definitely raise the energy on the dance floor, so be sure to add it to your arsenal.
