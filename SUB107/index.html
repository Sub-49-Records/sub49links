Once in a while we get a masterclass demo that breaks down genre boundaries and launches your body into the future where you’re grooving with extraterrestrials. “My Body” by Kinosia is one of those tracks that’ll make your body shuffle until you can’t move a muscle. Kinosia is an artist from Singapore currently living in Japan. He started producing in 2021, mainly making tech and deep house. He’s released on labels previously with this alt project, but this release would be considered his debut as Kinosia. Take a listen to this track that features a rhythmic but heavy bassline, deep house vocals surrounded by vocal chops, ear-candy FX, and hard-cutting percussions that blend together to make a tech - deep - bass house cocktail.
