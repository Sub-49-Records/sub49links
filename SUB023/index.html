Sub 49 is proud to present its first release of 2020, Flynninho's Wanna Rock n' Roll EP. Emerging from the Chicago underground, Flynninho is able to capture a lot of energy and emotion into his signature style of house - honoring the originators of Chicago house while bringing in the sound of the new era. We have strong faith you'll be hearing this EP in a number of environments and we are prepared to rinse it out at a club near you. Keep it locked - we are bringing you only the biggest and best in 2020.
