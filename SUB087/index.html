Let's dive deep into this case file. 'INVESTIGATE' by Tim Kietzmann is blend of Techno, Bass House, and G-House all in a tight package. Born in a small town in Germany in 1999, Tim Kietzmann has been in love with 4/4 music at an early age. He became fascinated by house music that had sonic characteristics of dark tones and heavy bass. This is evident in his track that utilizes an aggressive synth paired with a bouncy bass line. His newest track 'INVESTIGATE' combines an atmosphere of gloom with high energy bass stabs, along with powerful vocals that'll be sure to set the scene on the dance floor.
