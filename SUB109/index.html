There’s been a lot of talk around town that we have something special coming to the Sub 49 Discography. So let’s cut the chit chat and get straight into it. Sub 49 Records is proud to present “Chit Chat” by Average Citizens. This duo from Vancouver, B.C, named “Rising Artist” by DJ Mag, has quite the impressive resume. Having multiple #1 Beatport charting releases as well as being supported by the best of the best, including AC Slater, AC Slater, Dr. Fresch, Nostalgix, DJ Q, DJ EZ, Confession, EDM.com, and more. They’ve released on 3000 Bass, Jungle Cakes, WyldCard, YosH, Etiqueta Negra, Westwood X, InStereo Recordings, Brooklyn Fire, Ghetto Ghetto, UFO, Fort Knox Recordings, as well as Sheppard Records. Their songs have been placed in Spotify Editorial Playlist placement which you probably already have saved, Including Bangers, Mint, Fresh Finds, Fresh Finds Dance, and Shuffle Syndrome.

In line with their impressive release list, they’ve also played all over the world in places such as Spain, Mexico, Taiwan, England, Germany, El Salvador, Costa Rica, Ecuador and the USA. This includes 5 headline festivals, as well as playing at prolific festivals such as Shambhala, Wicked Woods, Foundation Series and FVDED in the Park.

And we haven't even gotten into the song yet, woof. “Chit Chat” is the epitome of a banging grimy UKG track that makes listeners shut up and dance. Featuring big wobbly basses, harsh but pleasant mid-high synths, percussions that keep you grounded, UK Vocals that’s nice with it, and fills that bring it all home just like your grandma's cookies.

You’d be quite silly to not add this to your DJ arsenal.

Please enjoy responsibly, and thank you for being a part of the Sub 49 nation.
