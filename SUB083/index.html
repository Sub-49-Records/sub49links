Sometimes we need to take a hard look at ourselves in the mirror and see our self-worth. The Me Myself and I EP by Leaky Eyes will make you feel, self-reflect, and groove. This track features jazzy soft chords that ride along swift percussions and sensual vocal chops. Hailing from Chiba, Japan, this 20 year old producer was influenced by artists such as Stevie Wonder, Sammy Virji, as well as Chris Lake. He brings in elements from Jazz and Disco Music to bring you a record that can be slipped into any UKG set.

Please enjoy responsibly, and thank you for being a part of the Sub 49 Nation.
