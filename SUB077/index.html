Grab a snack, sit down, and hold my beer. Brazilian producer, CHIPZ, is coming in hot with his new single, 'Beer', that features a grimey, catchy bassline with a groovy, techy backbone. Perfect for a prime-time tech house set, when the audience is craving a track with a bit of a bite. His 10 years of producing experience, along with his Greek heritage, shines through on this heater. Be sure to follow him and check out his other label releases on Subsense, Complex Destroyerz, Tiger Recordings, WyldCard and more.
