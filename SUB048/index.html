If the beat is sick, you better call the Witch Doctor. That's the title of Ronny Berna's latest release with Sub 49 - accompanied by a remix from the newcomer Nolephant. Menacing, dark, energetic - both of these tracks will trick you into thinking you're cured. We couldn't ask for a better pair of tracks to celebrate our 49th release. To everyone who has been supporting us since Day 1, thank you. We are privileged to be curating tunes for your crates and ears.
