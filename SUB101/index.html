'Lights, Camera, Action' as they say while we embrace our inner fanatics for our new song, 'Fanatic' by ScottyB. This track features a bangin groove, sultry vocals, along with a catchy melody to create a percussive track that is the recipe for fandom. Scott Banks, aka 'ScottyB,' began his musical journey in 2014 as a DJ in the local Los Angeles dance music scene, with a deep passion for trap and house music. Fast forward 10 years, ScottyB has curated his sound in high energy, house music. Today, ScottyB's productions focus on groovy drums, thumping bass and catchy leads. With recent releases on taste-maker labels such as HotCity, Boat House, and House Views, ScottyB is one to keep your eye on.
