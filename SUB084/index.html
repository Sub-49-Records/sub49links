The weekend is finally here. Break out the bell bottoms and meet us at studio 54. FEFO and Electric SIren are bringing you a record that is so fly you'll be sure to get all the jitterbugs out. FEFO (the GHOST), born and raised in the heart of Italy, seamlessly blends elements of tech house, bass house, and hip-hop with vintage melodies crafting a unique sonic tapestry. Electric Siren, hailing from America, brings in punchy drums, a deep bass, and a meaningful melody to draw in listeners like a siren luring sailors during ancient Greek times. The end result is the track 'Saturday Night Fever' that'll have everybody in the city boogie all the way down to the disco ball. Can you dig it?
