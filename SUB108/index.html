The only thing that can cure a witch's brew hangover is some Dance Serum. We got a brand new Seattle Artist to add to our Sub 49 cookbook. Zehr is a Seattle based producer that’s been on the rise in the local scene. Although he’s been producing for quite some time, He had his official debut release on Tacoma’s Early Night Records last year, and he’s been cooking up beats ever since. “Dance Serum” features a Tech House backbone with elements of Bass House, Brazilian Bass, and Zehr’s own flavor of house. The bassline synths are the secret ingredients in this track that’ll make tech house fans go for seconds. Be sure to add this tasty drink to your catalog and follow Zehr on his musical journey!
