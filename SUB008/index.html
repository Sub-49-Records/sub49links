Sub 49 is proud to present Subliminal Remixes Vol 1. As part of an ongoing series Subliminal Remixes, Vol. 1 includes fresh takes on the tracks from the first ever Sub 49 Records release, Subliminal Volume 1. Featuring artists on the original release GRAYMATTER, Jay Peeki, Floston, as well as more recent additions to the label, TV_TV, HAELIX, CITYWLKR, and YCHTCLB.
