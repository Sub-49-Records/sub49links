Wow wow wow. It took 5 years but we made it to our 100th release on Sub 49 Records. Looking back, there has been a lot of blood, sweat, and tears but from it, we've had a lot of amazing memories, music, and connections we are forever grateful to have. To celebrate 100 releases, we have our label manager, James Gatz with his new song 'I Just Wanna Know' releasing on his birthday 10/10/2024.

James Gatz has been known to push the boundaries of what is considered 4/4 music by blending multiple genres in his productions. His originals are a combination of Bass House, UKG, Tech House, Deep House, Future House, and Trap with oriental influences. His new track 'I Just Wanna Know' is no exception. This track features sultry vocals with atmospheric synths, along with vocal chops, bass hits, and hard-hitting percussions that come together to create a sonic journey. Let's give James Gatz and Sub 49 Records the congratulations they deserve.

Please enjoy responsibly, and thank you for being a part of the Sub 49 nation.
