Music Heals Our Souls, and our family is our Remedy. Sub 49 Records is excited to welcome back two of our beloved alumni’s, Myway (US) & Sadyouth with their track “Remedy”. This larger than life track is packed with festival friendly sounds that will tear up any dance floor around the world. With sultry vocals singing “Remedy”, synths that pluck the right melody, a big dirty bass that knocks your enemies, and a drum groove that vibrates your extremities, this is a festival weapon that you need in your crate. Sadyouth is a veteran in the scene, with releases on Hau5trap, Box of Cats, Hood Politics, North of Neptune, Jadu Dala and more, bouncing between Bass House, Tech House, UKG and whatever genre he’s feeling that day. A producer’s producer, he can make a banger in any format. Myway (US) is the youngest producer we’ve ever signed with releases on Retail Records, Get down, We are freaks, Sub 49, NV'd, Hot Fuss, and Municipal Records. He’s also been supported by CID, ESSEL, Piero Pirupa, Shiba San, Kamino, Black V Neck, & Giant all the while still being in his early 20’s. Looking at his resume, you know this guy is a star in the making.

Continuing our quest to make releases more memorable for artists, We will be hosting our 4th song release party of the year in Los Angeles with our good friends Thiccfreqness on July 18th, 2026 at an underground warehouse for a proper house music afters. Make sure to pre-save, get on the guest list, and we’ll see you in Los Angeles!

Please enjoy responsibly, and thank you for being a part of Sub 49 World Wide.
