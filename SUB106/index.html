We’re welcoming back DiscoTime to the Sub 49 catalog for the 3rd time with his track “Depend On Me”. Hailing from New York City, DiscoTime embarked on his music journey during the 2020 COVID Pandemic. He’s honed his craft and created his signature style of Tech House that features groovy basslines, hard percussive drums, and throwback synths to bring you a certified dance floor mover. He’s released on chapter eight records, as well as on Damon Sharpe’s label, Brainjack.

Please enjoy responsibly, and thank you for being a part of the Sub 49 nation.
