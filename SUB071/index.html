We're proud to present our latest up and coming producer, Taf Athorik, from Jakarta, Indonesia. Taf brings an irresistible groove to his production that will keep the dance floor groovin' all night long. 'ATKDEF' & 'Feel It' both share a perfect marriage between the kick and bass, along with stabs of quirky, piano samples, reminiscent of house juggernaut, Bleu Clair.
