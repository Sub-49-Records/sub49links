5-0 on our tail, Coms are hot, but we a quick learner. Ghost Mode switch the burner. Sub 49 Records is proud to present a truly one of a kind EP, Ghost Mode ft. MC Subdolo by Marzetti. For the first time in the history of this Seattle Record Label, We’ll be dropping a Grime track, a DnB Remix, UK Dub Remix, as well as 2 bangin Bass House + UKG Remixes. Introducing the pillar of this all-star line up, Marzetti, who’s an OG’s OG in the UK influenced house game. With decades of experience based out of Canada, and collaborations with heavy hitters such as Avicii, Ghettoblaster, Todd Terry, Dread MC plus countless others, he’s brought to our doorstep an original grime track with MC Subdolo that pays homage to what UK grime should be. Not only did he produce a serious rap heater, he remixed his own song, to deliver a UK Dub remix that even old heads yearn for. Looking at the list on this EP, there’s no wonder we said “YES”. Remixes from our new Sub 49 cohort, Average Citizens, their DnB counterpart, BRUKOUT [ca], as well as the hottest kid on the block, Coka Cobra. But we ain’t no snitches. Just take a listen for yourselves before we ditch this line. We gotta make steady calls to our earners. Ghost Mode Switch the Burner.
