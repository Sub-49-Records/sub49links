We’re kicking off the new year with a banging tech house release by ZUKO. ZUKO is a Latin Tech / Tech House DJ and producer who’s originally from Palm Springs, California. He started DJing when he was 12 years old, making it 16 years since he’s been DJing. After high school he moved to Seattle to expand his music career, since then he’s been on the come up from DJing at the hottest clubs in downtown and being direct support for big names in the scene. His track “Play Right” is a party anthem that is sure to be rinsed out by club DJ’s looking for a big hit on the dance floor. Be sure to follow ZUKO on his music journey.
