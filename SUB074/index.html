So, say goodnight to the bad guy...

Las Vegas original, now Seattle transplant, FAME Sounds, joins us for his second label release, overall, and first release with Sub 49! FAME Sounds began his career as a hip-hop and nightclub DJ, voted 'Best Nightclub DJ | Las Vegas 2014.' In 2015, FAME decided to take his journey north, to Seattle, and establish himself as an EDM DJ/Producer, creating the duo, DoBadlyy with another Seattleite. FAME, now, continues to release music under both monikers and is a highly sought after DJ in the Seattle music scene. We, at Sub 49, are proud to present FAME Sounds' newest song, 'Bad Guy' - a Tech House stomper that will surely get those feet movin' and those booties shakin'. Enjoy responsibly, and thank you for being a part of the Sub 49 Nation.
